The Story of Paradise Lost

This exhibit presents ten engraved illustrations from the 1794–1796 Parsons edition of John Milton's Paradise Lost. Together these images trace the poem's movement from heavenly rebellion through innocence in Eden, temptation, the Fall, and humanity's expulsion from Paradise.

The collection demonstrates how eighteenth-century artists transformed Milton's epic into visual form while also illustrating how descriptive metadata can support digital humanities research.
