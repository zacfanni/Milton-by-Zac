# Milton's Paradise Lost

This digital exhibit presents ten engraved illustrations from a late eighteenth-century edition of John Milton's *Paradise Lost* published by J. Parsons between 1794 and 1796.

Rather than simply reproducing illustrations, the exhibit explores how eighteenth-century artists visualized Milton's epic through scenes of temptation, celestial warfare, innocence, revelation, and exile.

Visitors may browse by:

* Book of Paradise Lost
* Characters
* Engraver
* Illustrator
* Date
* Themes

The collection also demonstrates how descriptive metadata supports digital humanities scholarship through searchable, interconnected objects.
